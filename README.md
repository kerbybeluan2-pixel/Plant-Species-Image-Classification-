### 2. Paeonia lactiflora
![Peony](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/bc36f4ba-3e1a-4c07-973f-321c7a57821f)
**Scientific Name:** *Paeonia lactiflora* (or *Paeonia suffruticosa* for tree peonies)
**Description:** A stunning flowering plant with large, full double blooms featuring soft pink to white petals. Peonies are perennial plants known for their longevity, fragrance, and garden prominence. They typically bloom in late spring to early summer and are popular in ornamental gardens and floral arrangements.

### 3. Cassia fistula
<img width="458" height="690" alt="3" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/691f22c7-6d76-4169-af91-5764680917c2" />
**Scientific Name:** *Cassia fistula*
**Description:** Commonly known as the Golden Shower tree, Cassia fistula is a tropical flowering plant renowned for its long, pendulous clusters of bright yellow flowers. Native to South Asia, this ornamental tree blooms during the summer months and produces distinctive long seed pods. It is valued for its striking golden blooms, making it a popular choice in tropical and subtropical gardens and landscapes.

### 4. Cercis canadensis
<img width="600" height="400" alt="4" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/a8d24f3e-5e8a-4f5f-8d8e-1234567890ab" />
**Scientific Name:** *Cercis canadensis*
**Description:** Commonly known as the Eastern Redbud or Judas tree, Cercis canadensis is a deciduous ornamental tree native to North America. It is famous for its stunning magenta or pink heart-shaped flowers that bloom directly on the branches in early spring before the leaves emerge. This small to medium-sized tree is valued for its attractive foliage, vibrant spring blooms, and adaptability to various growing conditions, making it a popular choice for gardens and landscapes.
